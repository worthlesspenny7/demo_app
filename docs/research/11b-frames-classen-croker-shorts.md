Scope: the 108 JPEGs in `docs/research/frames/` whose names start with `classen1-`, `croker2-`, `reading-`, `delay-`, `clock-`, `start-`, `makeup-`
(36 cues x 3 frames: "-3", "+0", "+4" seconds). Every frame was viewed (the "+0" first, the other two for every cue, because
most "+0" frames do not show the thing). Tiny handwriting was enlarged with ImageMagick for reading; no frame file was changed.

## 0. What the frames are (read this first)

1. **The Classen and Croker videos are a single fixed camera on the speaker at a lectern beside a flip chart. No slide, CAMEO, instruction page, chart
   or sign picture appears in any of the 69 frames** except: (a) the red Observation sign held up (02m40), (b) the flip-chart ASP arithmetic (12m30-+4, 26m40-28m29),
   (c) the flip-chart scoring example (19m50, 21m00), (d) a hand-drawn road/sign sketch on the flip chart (37m40 onwards, still there through Croker's part), (e) a flip-chart pad
   header "WELCOME GREAT RACE 2015" (04m30). Everything the transcripts say was "on the slides" (CAMEO #1-#76, hard left 25, snake left 20, emergency sheet, Column B/C of
   #1/#5, calibration blocks, #17, #26, #27/#33/#38, #61 clock with slash, the HB stop/corner charts, the paper time allowance sheets, the annotated transit rows) is **not visible in
   the supplied frames**. Section 2 transcribes only what is really there; where the transcripts' "what should be shown" has no pixel evidence the entry says so.
2. **The frame timestamps do not line up with the transcript timestamps** (the grabs were taken from the video timeline, the captions are 30 s blocks and the video appears to differ
   by roughly 2-9 minutes). Example: the scoring board that 10b places at 11:49-13:55 is on screen at video 19:50-21:00; the ASP board that 10b places at 10:14-11:17 is blank at 10:30 and
   half written at 12:30. Treat the file-name labels (taken from the transcript) as unreliable; the "What is actually visible" column below is the truth.
3. **Date evidence.** The flip-chart pad reads "WELCOME / GREAT / RACE / 2015" (04m30; only "LCOME / REAT / RACE / 015" is in frame), so the Classen/Croker videos are the **2015** rally school
   (10b said "year not stated"). Croker wears a lanyard badge with the number **70** (frames 09m30, 15m00, 22m10, 25m00). The Stumb "delay" video shows a **Coker Tire Challenge**
   sheet (c) 2014, "Saturday, September 20, 2014, Stage 2, Page 15 of 23" and the "reading" video shows the HB Appendix E **2014 Hagerty Trophy Run** (Ogunquit, Maine) pages, exactly the
   pages already transcribed in `08b-rookie-handbook-appendices.md` sec. 3.3. So the Stumb videos' page art adds only the handwriting and a second real sheet (Coker, Chattanooga).
4. The Stumb "how-to" series is filmed in two sets: a **brick wall** (clock, make-up) and a **museum with a Ford Tractor neon sign, a white 1950s Ford pickup and a car collection** (delay, start). Blue "Great Race" title cards with ghosted logo are used for text slides.
   The speaker is the same man in a black Great Race polo (the director, Jeff Stumb per 10c).

## 2. Exact transcriptions

### 2.1 Classen's ASP arithmetic board (classen1-12m30s +4; also lower half in 26m40-28m29)
Flip chart, black marker, left-aligned in the board's centre:
```
 11:30:00
 1h52m00            <- written "1h52m0 0" (the m is a small raised m)
 ----------------   (underline)
  1:22:00
  42m10s
 ----------------
  2:04:..           <- first of two lines, cut off by the lectern
  2:04:..           <- second line, cut off
```
Reading: start base 11:30:00 + ASP 112 minutes (1h52m00) = 1:22:00; + 42m10s (an interval to the first checkpoint) = 2:04:10 (the two "2:04" lines are the due time and, below,
the arrival time; the seconds are hidden). The 10:30 frames show the board blank, so the transcripts' "11:30 + 112 min = 1:22 pm" is right but it is **1h52m, not "112 min" written**; the board
shows hours/minutes with an `h` and an `m`.

### 2.2 Classen's scoring board (classen1-19m50s and 21m00s, all frames)
```
                                        4s early        <- small, top right, "45 early" at video resolution
   2:04:06
        3m09
   -------------------
   2:07:15
   2:07:20
                                  5s late               <- small, bottom right ("Ss late")
```
Reading: a leg ended at 2:04:06 (4 s early); the next leg's perfect time is **3m09 after the actual 2:04:06 = 2:07:15**; the car arrived 2:07:20 = 5 s late. The leg 2 clock restarts from the
car's actual crossing of checkpoint 1 (the leg reset, **SCORE-001: anchor_k = actual_k**), and the two errors do not cancel: 4 + 5 = 9 s (the transcript's "total 9" is not on the
board, it is spoken). The back sheet (curled over, right) has "Early" written on it.

### 2.3 The hand-drawn road sketch (classen1-37m40s through croker2-34m40s; sharpest in classen1-37m40s +0)
Pencil/marker on the flip chart, portrait page, in one continuous picture:
- A **main road comes up from the bottom** of the page as two long near-parallel thin lines (left edge bowing left, right edge nearly straight).
- Near the **upper left** the road divides: the left edge continues up-left as a curved line (a **left curve**); a **dashed line** runs down the middle of that curve and carries on down the middle of the road
  as a **dotted centre line** (dashes at the top, dots lower down) - this is the **centre stripe**, not a dashed-road symbol.
- A **narrow wedge/spur** forks off to the right between the curve and the straight road: two thin lines meeting at a point, with a **small circle (about the size of a knot) drawn on the spur** with a short tick above it (a **stop sign** on the side road).
- To the right of the main road, a **tilted square (diamond), rotated about 30 degrees**, containing a **thick arrow bent to the left at a right angle** (a curve/turn warning sign), with a thin leader line from its lower corner to the road.
- Under the diamond a small **rectangle with a number (illegible: "35" or "75")** = the plate ("35 MPH" advisory plate) under the warning sign.
- No labels except "Early" on the back sheet. This is the illustration of "the obvious principal road follows the centre stripe/curve" (10b A4 [29:32-31:38], #45 "follow this curve warning sign").
