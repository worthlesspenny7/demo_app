## 2. Where the videos contradict the documents (regulations win)
- Rookie discards: Jeff says 5 worst legs; REG I.F.3 says Rookie 6, Sportsman 5. Keep REG-003.
- Age factor "0.927 for a '63" in captions; REG V.D says 1954+ = 1.000. Keep the table.
- Paper TA sheets (Croker, 2024) vs web form (2026, REG). Default web; "classic paper" is an optional mode.
- Bluetooth headsets mentioned in 2026; REG II.H.1.g requires wired/tube. Keep REG.
- "Generally 15 s at stop signs" vs REG Example #4 stop without pause. Keep "only where printed", with the generator printing a pause on most STOPs.
- Clock: the handbook praises the Sawtooth clock; the director distrusts its minute hand. Both are allowed; the simulator models the loose minute hand and teaches the director's method.

## 4. Simulator changes implied (V3), ranked
1. Instrument model: analog clock with a loose minute hand (ambiguous within ~5 s of the minute change); "director's setup" = stopwatch TOD mode for time of day, clock for seconds; LESSON-006 rewritten; WATCH-009 accepts a stopwatch TOD read as a clock read; a one-minute-mistake trap in D16.
2. Start/restart procedure: position 1 at base + 1 min; launch at start time minus the standing-start net loss; 30-second warning and a countdown ending on the launch second; "do not pull up until the car ahead leaves" with other cars queued at the sign; nobody releases you.
3. TA flow: the 2026 web form fields (car number, password, phone, leg, instruction numbers, time in 10 s steps, red done button), measured delay = stopped time + chart loss, "make up the odd seconds" guidance, delay causes tractor/school bus/construction/combine, optional classic paper mode.
4. Make-up ledger: 20 % rule, running total in chunks, drop at the next speed change, stop shortening, "timed interval disturbed" warning.
5. Callout protocol in the driver model and LESSON-002: ICE, "mark", read-backs with "holding 35", the rock-back stop count with "keep counting".
6. Speeds 10-55 in charts; 48 as an assigned speed; 55 in warm-up/calibration/transit.
7. Checkpoint density 4-6 per day, one in the morning, one late surprise; the pace-car sanity check (the car one minute ahead/behind as the only live early/late cue).
8. Pre-read: checkpoint numbering and arrival-time notes in Column D; pre-written chart losses; D15 grades them.
9. Calibration: no live feedback; stock-speedo schedule correction ("1 s per N min") as a ledger aid; factor arithmetic as taught.
10. D06 chart tool: seconds-only entry, 4-run averages, outlier flag, per-driver charts, stop-in-the-middle variant.
11. Lost-time doctrine drill: turn-around stopwatch, double it, rejoin 30 s behind.
12. Timewise lesson and faults (low).
