# TSD (Time-Speed-Distance) Road Rally: Fundamentals and Newbie Advice

Research compiled 2026-10-03. Method note: the network egress proxy in this environment blocked direct page fetches for almost every rally-club / forum domain, so most content below was obtained via search-engine summaries of the cited pages (SCCA, PCA, JCNA, regional club PDFs, Grassroots Motorsports forum, etc.). Wording is paraphrased from those summaries unless shown in quotes; where a quote could not be verified against the original page it is flagged UNVERIFIED. Numeric tables that are pure arithmetic (60/speed) were computed and are marked as such.
