## 6. Verdict

Yes, and clearly closer than the V2 pass: 59/70 (was 54). The two things that cost most in V2 are gone. The clock is the Sawtooth of the video with no digital
readout and a loose minute hand, with the stopwatch's time-of-day mode as the director's source; and the printable page is, on the evidence of the five side-by-sides,
the organizers' page: column proportions to a point, bold centred Column C, calibration boxes, watch icons, odometer squares, three-block footer, rounded TA box,
Information Box, and at the legal rung 21-25 pages of 7-9 rows against the real 21-26. The start procedure follows the videos (position 1 = base + 1, nobody releases
you, pull up only after the car ahead has left, launch early by the net loss, a 30 s warning and a count that lands on the launch second), the make-up rules are exact
against the ghost, the calibration run gives no live feedback at any rung, the TA web form matches the 2026 frame and the committee credit floors to 10 s, scoring
caps, missed checkpoints, the DNF rule and the age factor reproduce the regulations, and the driver speaks ICE, "mark", "holding 35", "stopped" and "keep counting".
What still separates it from the real thing is content, not look: the calibration start prints the wrong "official" time, two seeds print a contradictory
time-left on the finish transit, generated stages are too regular (no blinker, no 10-15 mph, no posted limit below the assigned speed, no mid-stage exact transit,
no delayed or chained timed change), nine small slips remain (two low-medium: the Four S's "only a train or an accident" and the Reference's witness field on the web
form), and the score ladder still has no 20-46 s rookie. Fixes 1-5 are small and would take the page and the lessons to near parity.
